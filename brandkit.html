01 Principles what the brand stands for

The idea

Everything is built from four shapes on warm paper. Circles, squares, a dotted trail, and a crosshair. Nothing decorative — every mark means something. Minimalism as gameplay, geometry as language.

Three rules

1 — Paper & ink only; blue means something.
2 — Draw with the four primitives, nothing else.
3 — Numbers are monospaced and never jump.
