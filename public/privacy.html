SPACE SWOOSH · SPACESWOOSH.APP

Privacy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Space Swoosh (“the game”) is a one-thumb dodge game published at spaceswoosh.app and as native apps for iOS and Android. This page explains what data we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Leaderboard call sign and score. If you choose to submit a high score, we store the name you enter, your distance, and obstacles destroyed. This is optional.
  • Purchase history. Optional cosmetic ship unlocks are processed by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat. We receive entitlement status so the app can unlock what you bought — not your full payment card details.
  • Local settings. Ship choice, sound mute, Journey progress, Open World personal best, and owned skins are stored on your device (localStorage / app preferences).
  • Analytics (web only). The browser version may use Google Analytics to count visits and basic events (for example game over). The native apps do not load that script.

What we do not do

  • We do not create accounts or require email login to play.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your data for cross-app tracking advertising.

Third parties

  • Supabase — leaderboard database.
  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase receipt validation (native apps).
  • Google Analytics — web analytics only.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — billing when you unlock a cosmetic ship.

Children

The game is a simple skill game with no chat. Leaderboard names are filtered for obvious abuse. If you believe a name violates our rules, email us.

Your choices

  • Do not submit a score if you prefer not to share a call sign.
  • Clearing app data / site storage removes local settings and cached unlocks (store purchases can be restored via Options → Restore Purchases).
  • To request deletion of a leaderboard entry that contains your call sign, contact us with the name and approximate score.
